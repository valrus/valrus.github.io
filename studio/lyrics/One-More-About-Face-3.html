One More About Face 3

What’s one more about face?
You think everything’s ruined,
but you’re doing what you can with what you have,
and that’s enough.
You and I both know your life
and see the thread that’s running through it,
and one more about face
isn’t going to do it any harm.

What’s one more about face?
I’ve never been more certain:
you’re drawing back the curtain on a version of yourself
I’m the first in line to see!
So you deserve no worse from me
than a sturdy place to stand and plan one more about face.
